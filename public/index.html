The Story

You're a Hogwarts student trying to pass your classes. Your professors hand you tasks like brew this, read that, cast the other, and every task has to be finished before the bell rings.

Each classroom is its own level of chaos: bubbling cauldrons in Potions, tea leaves and crystal balls in Divination, and loose pixies in Defense Against the Dark Arts. Complete tickets, earn house points, and bring your house to the top before the end of the term.

It's a tight loop with intuitive mechanics and a clear goal.

Features

  • Three classrooms as levels: Potions, Divination, Defense Against the Dark Arts.
  • Procedurally generated task tickets handed out each round.
  • Core actions: pick up, put down, use (brew, cut, cast at stations), combine.
  • Complete all tasks before the timer runs out.
  • Fixed isometric camera - the whole classroom is visible at a glance.
  • Pixie hazard - Pixies that freezes the player on collision.
  • House points & stars tracked across all classrooms toward the end-of-term score.
  • Persistent game state that survives every scene change.
Game loop:  spawn → receive ticket → pick up + place + use + combine → hand in → get points → timer runs out → end of level → score → continue or repeat

Potions Class

Brew bubbling concoctions at the cauldron stations.

Divination Class

Read tea leaves, cards, and the crystal ball. Stations: séance table, teapot, card table, bookshelf, brazier, delivery desk. Over-steeped tea = chaos!

Defense Against the Dark Arts

Cast charms and wrangle loose pixies before the professor returns.

Scene flow and the characters inside each level.

Scene ↔ character relationships

  • Student (Player): the only player-controlled character; present in the corridor hub and every classroom. Animations: Idle, Walk, Cast Spell.
  • Professor (NPC): lives inside a classroom; hands out task tickets and receives the finished products.
  • Cornish Pixie (only in DADA): moves through the classroom on a procedural pattern and freezes the player on collision; it remains only in DADA.
  • Hogwarts corridor (hub): connects the three classroom scenes; the player can go back and forth between them.
  • GameState: not a scene but a persistent object (DontDestroyOnLoad) that every scene reads and writes: house points, stars earned, progress.
